Ride-to-the-Polls Module

The production version can connect this button to a secure ride-request form or dispatch partner. It should collect only the information necessary to provide transportation and should never ask how someone intends to vote.

Recommended fields: first name, phone, pickup ZIP/area, requested time window, mobility/accessibility accommodation, and consent to be contacted.
